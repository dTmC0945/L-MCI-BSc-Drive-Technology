* Lecture Information

** Lecture Overview

- The goal of this lecture is to give an introduction to the fascinating world
  of electric machines and relevant mathematical methods.
- We will start from understanding how machines work to the types currently used
  in commercial and industrial applications.
- The lecture will focus on mathematical and engineering concepts which will
  have direct use with real-life engineering applications.

** Requirements and the Learning Outcomes

- The student should have a strong background in circuit analysis and
  familiarity with integral/differential calculus.
- After completion, the student will have a good grasp of electrical machines
  from their construction, how they are designed, what types out there and what
  applications are possible.
** Grading of the Lecture


- The lecture will have a single personal assignment comprising of a set list of
  questions and a final exam comprising of all the topics covered in the
  lecture.
  - Additional instructions for both will be given in the lecture(s).
- For the written exam you are allowed to write your personal equation reference
  paper, as long as it is a single sheet of A4, double sided and contains no
  exercise or solutions.
  - Your reference sheet will be collected after the exam.
